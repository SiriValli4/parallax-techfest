Techfest, IIT Bombay is Asia's largest science and technology festival, bringing together students, researchers, innovators and industry leaders for an unparalleled celebration of ideas.

From high-octane robotics battles to international research showcases, guest lectures by Nobel laureates, and hackathons that run through the night — Techfest is a launchpad for the next generation of builders and dreamers.
